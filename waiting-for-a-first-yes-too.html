We Built This Waiting for a First Yes. Now We're Waiting Too.

Volunteer Team · 2026-09-07

This is a little "meta," small m. We built a tool for campaigns stuck at zero donations, and our own fundraiser to keep that tool running is currently sitting at zero donations.

We built Boost Board over a Labor Day weekend, put together by volunteers, several of whom are personally dealing with their own employment struggles right now. That's not a coincidence — it's a lot of why this exists at all. And if we're honest, everyone we know is a little tapped out on compassion right now. Asking anyone for anything feels harder than it used to.

The honest part

Faith that things will work out is a lot easier to hold onto when your own basic needs are met. We know that. We're not asking anyone to give from a place they cannot. Sometimes even sharing is a lot. Our own campaign might stay parked at zero for a while. It might not. Genuinely hard to say.

The optimistic part

Here's what we keep coming back to: even a dollar, even five dollars, matters more than it sounds like it should — especially from someone who feels compelled to act rather than someone just going through the motions. The data backs this up too: a single share alone tends to move real money, and being someone's actual first donor changes how a campaign gets seen from that point forward, not just how much it's raised.

We built this because we believed campaigns stuck at zero deserved a real shot at being seen. Relatable? Much.
