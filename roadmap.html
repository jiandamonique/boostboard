Where this is headed

Roadmap

We're still figuring out the longer-term direction of this project, so we don't want to make promises here we might not keep.

Right now, our focus is squarely on two things: making the core tool work well (reviewing submissions, keeping the board fair and accurate) and outreach — actually reaching the people and communities this is meant to help.

Bigger questions — what this becomes, whether it grows beyond what it is today — are genuinely still open. We'd rather be honest about that than sketch out a future we're not sure of yet.
