Labor Day Launch: Boost Board Gives Daily Visibility to Fundraising Campaigns Stuck at Zero

Press Release · 2026-09-07

Boost Board hand-reviews and rotates a spotlighted campaign each day, aiming to fix a problem every crowdfunding platform shares: good campaigns that nobody outside the organizer's own circle ever sees.

Among the site's first goals: helping the volunteers behind it pay for proper web hosting and fund further development of the tool.

September 7, 2026 — BERKELEY, Calif. — Boost Board, a free tool built and run by volunteers, today highlighted its growing effort to boost daily visibility to fundraising campaigns that haven't received their first donation—including the site itself. At the time of this writing, the site's campaign is itself "stuck at zero." The site — live at jiandamonique.github.io/boostboard — is in beta.

How it works

Boost Board reviews every submission provided and automatically rotates a featured campaign daily. Qualifiers have to have no or low-percentage donations. Fundraisers that would otherwise stay buried are signal boosted in this manner, just like any other user on social or offline would signal boost with a share. Same idea. The board provides another chance to be seen by people outside the organizer's immediate network.

Boost Board isn't a donation platform itself. It doesn't collect, hold, or process any money on behalf of the campaigns it features — visitors who choose to give click through to a campaign's own page and donate there directly. Boost Board's role is strictly amplification, aka #signalboost.

All of Boost Board's current examples happen to be GoFundMe campaigns, but that's a reflection of what's been submitted so far, not a platform requirement — Boost Board is built to feature fundraisers from any platform with its own hosted campaign page. For now, the team is holding off on featuring bare direct-payment links (a Venmo, CashApp, or similar handle with no campaign page attached) — vetting a request with no story, history, or independent verification behind it isn't yet a solved problem, and Boost Board would rather grow slowly and carefully than get that wrong.

Every submission is reviewed before it's featured, checking that a campaign is genuine, still sitting at zero donations (or close to it), and safe to share. The nature of reviewing and sharing campaigns stimulates activity, so by the time the campaigns are posted, the staff has found that few remain at zero. This is a good thing. By design, however, campaigns move through simple visibility tiers as they pick up support — "Seed" (0 donors), "First Five" (1–4 donors), and "First Ten" (5–9 donors) — before rotating off the board so the spotlight moves to whoever needs it next.

"We are our own beta testers," said Jianda Monique, co-founder of Boost Board. "We have volunteers who need help finding work and paying for living expenses, and our own campaign is currently at zero dollars."

"We're releasing this on Labor Day, a holiday about the dignity of paid work, because right now the people building this tool are doing it unpaid — for people who can't find paid work either," Monique added.

The same zero-donation problem Boost Board was built to solve is one its own team is living through right now, both personally and in funding the tool itself. Boost Board has launched its own GoFundMe campaign to cover the costs of keeping the project running — hosting, a small stipend for the volunteers, for development work as well (UI, UX, and infrastructure). Beyond those day-to-day costs, funds raised will also help Boost Board move off free static hosting onto a proper web host, grow the project into a full app, and expand its reach to more communities and campaigns. None of those funds go toward the campaigns featured on the board itself, which visitors are encouraged to support first and foremost.

Boost Board also publishes a free, no-signup RSS feed of the daily featured campaign, and offers free tools — a shareable progress graphic, a QR code generator, and an embeddable widget — for any fundraiser to use, regardless of which platform they're on. As it's a newly-launched endeavor, bug reports can be submitted via github or via the press contact indicated in this writing.

  • Can't give right now? A share helps just as much — that's the whole idea behind crowdfunding: small contributions, from enough people, add up to something real.

About Boost Board

Boost Board is a small, volunteer-run app that gives daily visibility to fundraising campaigns that haven't gotten their first donation yet. It is not a charity or nonprofit, and it never touches or processes donations for the campaigns it features — it exists solely to help real needs be seen by more people.
