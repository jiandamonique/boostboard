For organizers & people nominating a campaign

Help Us Help You

Boost Board is for campaigns with no funding or only a small part of their goal raised — not campaigns already riding a wave of attention.

Have these details ready

  • The original, public campaign link
  • What it's raising money for and why it matters
  • Roughly how many donors it has so far
  • Whether you're the organizer or suggesting it

Occasionally a campaign is pinned as the featured spot for a set window of time — a few days, for example — rather than through the usual daily rotation. Once that window ends, it returns to normal rotation on its own; no separate step needed on our end.

A submission is a suggestion for a spotlight — there's no guaranteed placement or timeframe. If featured, keep the original campaign's story and updates accurate, since that's what people will read.

Before you get started.

The practical details about sharing, submitting, and supporting.

Is there a funding cutoff for submissions?

Our focus is campaigns at zero or with only a small part of their goal raised. If a campaign has stalled early, share its goal, current total, and context when submitting.

Can I suggest a campaign I don't run?

Yes. Submit the original public link and say that you aren't the organizer. Don't include private contact details or personal information without permission.

Should I share the Boost Board page or the fundraiser?

Use the original fundraiser's link when asking people to read the full story or contribute — it gives them the latest updates and the platform's donation process in one place.

Where does my donation go?

You contribute on the campaign's original fundraising platform, not Boost Board. That platform manages payments under its own terms and payout policies.

Will submitting guarantee my campaign gets funded?

No. Submission doesn't guarantee a listing, and a listing doesn't guarantee donations. Boost Board helps with discovery and sharing; the decision to support belongs to each person who reads your story.

Something's wrong with a listed campaign, or it's not at zero anymore — what do I do?

Use the "Report / Update" link on that campaign's card on the board, and we will review it. We appreciate any help you can provide.
