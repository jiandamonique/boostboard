Our First Yes

Volunteer Team · 2026-10-08

Someone gave us a boost. 🙏

$100 came in, and we wanted to say something about it here. Thank you thank you! We wrote a whole post about sitting at zero — about knowing exactly how it feels to wait for a first donation, since we watch campaigns in that exact spot every single day. And then, quietly, one person gave.

$100 won't cover everything, but it's more than enough to inch forward. It goes toward a real domain and proper hosting — moving Boost Board off the long GitHub URL and onto something more shareable and stable. That's the first step toward everything else we want to build.

If you've been thinking about it, this is a good moment. Every dollar gets added to what's already there. And if giving isn't possible, sharing the board with one person who might connect with a campaign on it does real work too.

To our first donor: thank you. You know what a first donation means. So do the 50 campaigns currently on this board.
