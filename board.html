A campaign moves through these tiers as it picks up support, then rotates off the board once it passes 10 donors — the spotlight moves on to whoever needs it next.

A couple of exceptions, on purpose: we'll sometimes keep a campaign visible past 10 donors if it's barely made a dent in a much larger goal, or if our team has flagged it as getting very little reach despite the donor count.

Our site is new and we're still putting the pieces together — none of this is a hard and fast rule. The goal is just to help signal-boost, and to make that systematic instead of random. <3

This project is run by volunteers. If a campaign hasn't rotated through yet, please keep reaching out to your own community too — we want to support your success. To that end, please read some helpful ideas here.

Every campaign here is reviewed by hand before it's added, which takes time — so by the time one appears, it may already have picked up a donation or two elsewhere. That's a genuinely good sign, not a mistake. If you notice something that looks off, the "Report / Update" link on that card is the easiest way to let us know.

If your situation is urgent, please don't wait on us. Try posting on social media with #BoostBoard or #SignalBoost to get your campaign some immediate attention. We're sorry that we don't have the staff to respond to urgent needs in real time.

Boost Board isn't a charity or nonprofit — it's just an app.

Volunteers run it, it isn't tax-deductible, and it only signal boosts donations randomly, as submitted or brought to our attention. If you'd like to support the effort, you've got two options: help keep the app itself running, or send your money straight to today's featured campaign instead.
