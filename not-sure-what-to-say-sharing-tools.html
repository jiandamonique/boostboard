Not Sure What to Say? Try These Ideas

Volunteer Team · 2026-09-09

Knowing you want to share a campaign and knowing what to write are two different problems. The first is easy — the second stops a lot of people before they ever hit post. So we built a few small tools directly into Boost Board to close that gap, and we wanted to walk through what they do.

A starting point for your message

On the How You Can Help page, there's an editable message template already written — you're not starting from a blank box:

"This campaign caught my attention because [your reason]. It's still looking for early support. If it resonates with you, take a look or pass it along: [campaign link]"

Replace the brackets with your own words and the actual campaign link, and it's ready. The copy button is a little smarter than a plain copy-paste, too — it checks whether you've replaced the bracketed parts, and reminds you to finish before sharing if you haven't.

A self-check, not a gate

Right below the message template sits a short checklist — three quick boxes: that you've read the actual campaign, that your message sticks to what the organizer said, and that there's no pressure attached. Checking them doesn't unlock anything or block the share button. It's just there because a ten-second pause before posting tends to make the message better.

A multi-select share list on the board

If more than one campaign catches your attention, the board now lets you select a few at once — a checkbox on each card — and generates one message covering all of them, ready to copy or share directly through your phone's own share sheet if it supports that.

None of this is required

Every one of these tools is optional. A plain link, pasted with no template at all, still helps just as much. These exist for the moments when you want to share something but the blank box is what's stopping you — which, realistically, is most of the time for most people.

Keep it personal, factual, and pressure-free — the same three words on the page itself. That's really the whole guidance.
