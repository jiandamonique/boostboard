Common Fundraising Platforms

Volunteer Team · 2026-09-08

Boost Board doesn't require any specific platform — bring whichever one you're already using. This is just a plain reference, not an endorsement of any one over another.

GoFundMe

Donation-based, widely used for personal and medical fundraisers. No reward tiers required.

Indiegogo / Kickstarter

Reward-based, built for products and creative projects rather than personal needs — usually not the right fit for an individual crisis fundraiser, but common for community projects.

Venmo / CashApp / PayPal.me

Direct payment handles, no campaign page or story attached. Lowest setup effort — Boost Board accepts these as-is.

Ko-fi / GiveSendGo / Fundly

Smaller donation-based platforms, similar in spirit to GoFundMe with different fee structures — worth comparing if fees are a concern.

Gumroad

Built for selling digital products or creative work directly, but also commonly used as a simple way to accept direct support — worth including if that's what someone's already set up.

Fees, payout times, and identity verification requirements vary and change — check each platform's own site for current details rather than relying on this page for that. This list also lives as a standalone reference on the Campaign Tools page.
