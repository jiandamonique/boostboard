13 of 25: What Progress on Zero Looks Like

Volunteer Team · 2026-09-14

What "13 of 25" means

There were 25 campaigns on the board, and 13 of them — just over half — hadn't gotten a single donation yet. Boost Board exists to find exactly those campaigns, so a high number here is the goal. The more of the board that qualifies as "still at zero," the better the sourcing is working.

How campaigns move through the board

Every campaign passes through four simple stages as it picks up support:

Seed (zero donations) — 13 First Five (1-4 donations) — 7 First Ten (5-9 donations) — 4 Graduated (10+ donations) — 1

"Graduated" means a campaign got 10 or more donations and moved off the board to make room. One campaign had already done that. That's the goal for every campaign here — we want the board to lose people to success. And once a campaign graduates, it can still use more support; it just isn't sitting at zero anymore.

How the board got to 25

It took about a week: 15 campaigns to start, then a few smaller batches of 2, 2, and 6 as more were found. It would have been faster to add a big list all at once, but each of these 25 was checked one at a time to make sure it was real. That's slower on purpose.

11 kinds of need, not just one

Housing, medical bills, family support, financial hardship, education, animals, international aid, community projects, legal fees, sports, and crisis relief — all 11 had at least one campaign on the board. People need help for all kinds of reasons, so the board tries to reflect that instead of only featuring one type of story.

None of this changes without a real donation or a real share. This count just shows there are plenty of zero-donation campaigns to find — what happens next is up to you.

If you want to see who's currently in that 13, the board shows everyone in rotation right now, oldest zero-donation campaigns first.
