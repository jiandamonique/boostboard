Zero Hour...Continues

Volunteer Team · 2026-09-16

We aren't in any realistic position to do capacity building right now — small volunteer staff, no funding, little infrastructure. What we can do is build something small alongside other people, the same quiet way small groups already signal boost each other on social media every day. That's what Boost Board is: a handful of volunteers, adding GoFundMes and other campaigns one at a time as they come in, trying to get a little more attention to campaigns that haven't gotten any yet.

A quick note on that: GoFundMe shows up most often here simply because it's the name most people already know for this kind of campaign — like saying "Kleenex" or "Xerox" instead of "tissue" or "copy" , in a sense — not because we're exclusive to it. We welcome campaigns from any platform, and we don't represent GoFundMe or any other platform in any way.

It's slow going. We keep adding campaigns and gathering submissions, and the board grows a little more each week — but a little is the operative word. We're still early. And our own campaign, the one that keeps this whole thing running, is sitting at exactly zero donations, right alongside the campaigns we're trying to help. There's something fitting about that, even if it's not exactly comfortable to say out loud.

Building slowly, in public, without pretending to be further along than we are, still feels like the right way to do this. It just means asking for help along the way isn't optional — it's the actual model.

If any of this resonates, the most useful things you can do right now: share a campaign that's stuck at zero, submit one you know about, or — if you're able — help us keep the lights on.
