Campaign Tools

A progress graphic and QR code for your fundraiser — works for any platform.

These numbers are self-reported and only update when you regenerate this page — there's no live connection to any fundraising platform (none of them offer one). Update and re-download whenever your total changes.
Download QR code

QR code is generated by a free third-party service (api.qrserver.com) — if the "Download" button opens a new tab instead of saving, right-click the image and choose "Save image as."

Embed a Boost Board widget on your own site

Paste this into your own site to show a small, live campaign card — either today's featured campaign, or a different random one on every page load.
