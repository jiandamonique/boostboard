Past the first donation

How You Can Help

A campaign getting its first donor is the beginning of the goal, not the end of it.

We don't want this to stop at one donation. Please keep spreading the word — about the campaign that's featured, and about Boost Board itself — even after someone's given. Statistically, sharing helps almost as much as the donations do.

The numbers, briefly

3× more likely Campaigns shared more than six times in their first few days were three times as likely to go on to raise significantly more than campaigns shared less often.
Source: "The State of Modern Philanthropy 2023," Classy and GoFundMe.
5% more money, platform-wide GoFundMe's own internal testing found that getting just 10 percentage points more of its campaigns shared at all produced a 5% increase in total donation volume across the whole platform.
Source: GoFundMe's product analytics case study, published by Amplitude.
257% jump A separate study found that getting a crowdfunding campaign in front of people who don't already know the organizer — not just their existing circle — increased donations by 257%, almost all of it from people with no personal connection to the campaigner at all.
Source: peer-reviewed crowdfunding visibility study (Qingsongchou).

More detail and sourcing on our "Sharing Does More Than You Think" post.

What that means, concretely

A share takes fifteen seconds and costs nothing. Based on the numbers above, that fifteen seconds is doing real, measurable work — for a campaign you already donated to, or one you can't afford to donate to at all. Both matter equally here.
