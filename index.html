More about the process
Does Boost Board collect donations?

No. Boost Board helps people discover overlooked crowdfunding campaigns. Any contribution happens on the campaign's original fundraising platform, under that platform's own terms and fees.

What does "zero or close to it" mean?

Our focus is campaigns that haven't found their first supporters, or have raised only a small part of their goal. "Zero" is really shorthand for low activity, not always a literal $0.

Can I help without spending money?

Absolutely. Sharing a campaign is genuinely, measurably helpful, not a consolation prize -- see the research.

Does a feature mean a campaign is verified?

No. Being featured is not a guarantee of a campaign's claims or outcome. Read the original listing and check the fundraising platform's protections before giving.

Can I submit someone else's campaign?

You can suggest a publicly available campaign. Make clear that you aren't the organizer, and don't include private information without permission.
