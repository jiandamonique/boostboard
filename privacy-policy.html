Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-07

What we collect

When you submit a campaign, we ask for an email address so we can follow up with you — to approve your submission, ask a clarifying question, or let you know when you've been featured. We also ask for your name, but that field is optional. We don't ask for anything else about you personally; the campaign details you share (name, description, link) come from your already-public fundraiser page.

If you sign up to get an email when a new campaign hits zero donations, we collect just the email address you provide for that purpose.

What we don't do

We don't sell, share, rent, or otherwise pass your email address (or name, if given) to any third party for any purpose. We don't use it for marketing beyond what you specifically signed up for. We don't add campaign submitters to any mailing list they didn't separately opt into.

How long we keep it

If you submitted a campaign: we keep your email (and name, if given) until your campaign has been featured and we've sent you a courtesy note letting you know — or until we decide not to feature it, if that happens sooner. After that, we delete it. If we're actively investigating a report related to your campaign, we may retain it a bit longer while that's ongoing.

If you signed up for zero-donation alerts: we keep your email until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it.

Reports

If someone reports a campaign, we review it manually. We don't publish who filed a report or share that information with the reported campaign.

Volunteer-run, plainly stated

This project is run by a small team of volunteers, not a company. We built this policy to reflect what we actually do, not to sound official — if anything here ever stops being true in practice, we'll fix the practice or update this page, not the other way around.

Questions
