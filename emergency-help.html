If you need help faster than a rotating board

Emergency & mutual aid resources

Boost Board rotates campaigns daily, which isn't fast enough for every situation. These are places that may be able to help sooner, depending on where you live.

If you're in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services directly rather than relying on any resource on this page.

988 (United States)

  • The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org — for anyone in emotional distress or suicidal crisis, or worried about someone who might be.
  • Free, confidential, and available 24/7.

211 (United States)

  • A free, confidential helpline — dial 211, or visit 211.org — that connects you to local help with housing, utilities, food, and other essential needs.
  • Available 24/7 in most areas, with interpretation in many languages.

Local mutual aid networks

  • Mutual Aid Hub (mutualaidhub.org) maintains a directory of local mutual aid groups across the US — searchable by location.
  • Many cities have their own mutual aid networks (search "[your city] mutual aid") that move faster than any national platform, since they're run by people nearby.

Community-specific resources

  • If your situation relates to domestic violence, housing loss, disability, or another specific circumstance, an organization focused on that exact issue often has faster, more targeted help than a general fundraising platform.

Availability, eligibility, and wait times vary by location and change over time — please verify current details directly with each organization rather than relying solely on this page.
